Complete Control Over Your Drum Mics

BleedShift 2 gives engineers and producers unprecedented control over microphone bleed in multi-miked drum recordings. With our separation algorithm, you can now selectively reduce cymbal bleed in your tom mics, snare in your kick, or any other combination - all while maintaining the natural tone and character of your drums.

BleedShift 2 solves one of the most common and frustrating problems in drum recording, allowing you to shape your drum mix with surgical precision without the artifacts and phase issues of traditional gates and filters. As a standalone application, it's easy to use and integrate into your workflow.
